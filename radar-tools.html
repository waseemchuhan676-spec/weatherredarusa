Air Quality Index (AQI) Guide

EPA Air Quality standard reference to protect your respiratory health from wildfire smoke and ozone.

0-50 (Good): Air quality is satisfactory and poses little risk.
51-100 (Moderate): Acceptable quality; slight concern for sensitive people.
101-150 (Sensitive): Children and respiratory patients may experience symptoms.
151-200+ (Unhealthy): Everyone should limit prolonged outdoor exertion.
