About Weather Radar USA

Real-time meteorological tracking built for clarity, speed, and severe storm awareness.

Weather Radar USA was founded with a straightforward mission: to provide the fastest, cleanest, and most intuitive live weather radar experience for residents, travelers, outdoor enthusiasts, and emergency planners across the United States.

Commercial weather portals have become cluttered with video popups, heavy scripts, and slow-loading bloat. When severe weather threatens your community, you need instant radar access — clear Doppler loops, immediate storm vectors, and active National Weather Service alerts without delay.

Our Meteorological Data Sources

We believe in complete transparency about where our weather data originates. Our application interfaces with reputable governmental and meteorological API endpoints:

📡 NOAA NEXRAD WSR-88D

High-resolution Doppler radar reflectivity scans from more than 160 National Weather Service radar installations across the USA.

🚨 National Weather Service (NWS)

Real-time active emergency alert feeds including Tornado Warnings, Severe Thunderstorm Warnings, Flash Flood Advisories, and Winter Storm Watches.

🌦️ Open-Meteo & RainViewer

High-performance global weather forecast models and animated radar tile caches updating every 5 to 10 minutes.

Key Features We Deliver

Our platform is engineered using modern web technologies to run smoothly on desktop, tablet, and smartphone browsers:

  • Continuous 2-Hour Animated Radar Loop: See storm trajectory, speed, and cell intensification in real time.
  • Interactive Street-Level Zoom: Pan and zoom down to your specific neighborhood or county.
  • City & 5-Digit ZIP Search: Instant temperature, real-feel, wind gusts, hourly forecast, and 7-day outlook.
  • Real-Time US Air Quality Index (AQI): EPA-standard PM2.5 and Ozone health tracking.
  • Severe Storm Preparedness: Curated recommendations for emergency NOAA weather radios and storm backup equipment.
