🌦️ Tampa Weather & Live Doppler Radar Guide

Tampa Bay sits along Florida's west-central Gulf Coast in what is widely recognized as the lightning capital of North America. The Tampa metropolitan area features a humid subtropical climate dominated by intense afternoon convective thunderstorms from May through October, driven by the collision of Atlantic and Gulf of Mexico sea-breeze fronts. Real-time Doppler weather radar is vital for tracking rapid storm buildup, severe lightning strikes, localized flash flooding in Hillsborough and Pinellas counties, and approaching tropical systems from the Gulf.

📡 NOAA NEXRAD Station: KTBW (Ruskin / Tampa Bay, FL)

Our live Tampa radar map connects directly to NOAA's Level-III NEXRAD radar station KTBW, situated in Ruskin, Florida (south of downtown Tampa). Operating with S-band dual-polarization technology, station KTBW detects both horizontal and vertical pulse reflections, allowing meteorologists to discern torrential tropical downpours from hail cores and storm debris across west-central Florida and the eastern Gulf of Mexico.

🎨 How to Read Doppler Reflectivity (dBZ Scale)

Radar reflectivity measures precipitation density in decibels of Z (dBZ). Use this color reference when monitoring approaching storms on the Tampa radar:

15-30 dBZ: Light Rain
35-45 dBZ: Moderate Rain
50-55 dBZ: Heavy Storms
60+ dBZ: Hail / Severe

⚠️ Seasonal Severe Storm Threats in Tampa

During the Atlantic hurricane season (June 1 through November 30), Tampa Bay faces serious vulnerabilities to tropical storms and major hurricanes producing destructive storm surge, catastrophic rainfall, and embedded tornadoes. Summer afternoons regularly bring severe pulse thunderstorms with wind gusts over 55 mph and intense cloud-to-ground lightning.

Frequently Asked Questions: Tampa Weather Radar

Essential questions answered regarding Tampa Doppler radar data, NOAA NEXRAD coverage, and severe storm tracking.

How do I track live Doppler weather radar for Tampa Bay, FL?
You can track live high-definition Tampa Bay weather radar right here on Weather Radar USA. Our interactive Doppler map updates every 5 to 10 minutes with precipitation loops, storm velocity, rain intensity, and official NOAA NWS storm alerts for Tampa, St. Petersburg, and Clearwater.
Which NOAA NEXRAD radar station covers Tampa and Hillsborough County?
The primary National Weather Service Doppler radar serving the Tampa Bay region is station KTBW, located in Ruskin, Florida. It operates with high-frequency S-band dual-polarization technology to monitor tropical systems and severe thunderstorms within a 230-nautical-mile radius.
Why does Tampa experience so many summer thunderstorms?
Tampa's unique peninsula geography causes daily afternoon sea breezes from both the Gulf of Mexico and the Atlantic Ocean to collide over central Florida. This convergence lifts warm, moisture-rich tropical air, triggering explosive thunderstorm clusters almost every summer afternoon.
How do I check active hurricane and flood warnings for Tampa, FL?
Active National Weather Service warnings for Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco counties are automatically streamed directly above our radar map. You can also view active warning polygons and detailed bulletins on our NWS Severe Alerts feed.
