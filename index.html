How to Use Live Weather Radar in the USA

Weather radar (also called Doppler radar or NEXRAD) is the main tool meteorologists use to track precipitation and severe storms across the United States in real time. The National Weather Service operates more than 160 WSR-88D radar sites that provide coverage for nearly the entire country.

Search by city or ZIP code

Type a city name (for example “Austin TX”) or a 5-digit ZIP code in the search box above. Select from the suggestions or press Search. You will receive current temperature, humidity, wind, US Air Quality Index, sunrise and sunset times for that exact location.

Why check the national mosaic

The national live weather radar map shows the big picture of storms moving across the country. Use it together with local station links when severe weather is expected in your region.

Our recommendations for safer planning

Before outdoor activities Check both the local radar and the Air Quality Index. Clear skies with high AQI can still affect sensitive groups.
Severe weather When storms approach, open the official NWS radar and look at velocity products for rotation.
Best timing Radar is most useful 30–90 minutes before rain or storms are expected to reach your area.
Sunrise & sunset Use the times returned by search for photography, hiking or commuting. They are calculated for the exact coordinates.

Understanding the US Air Quality Index

  • 0–50 Good – Air quality is satisfactory
  • 51–100 Moderate – Acceptable for most people
  • 101–150 Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups
  • 151–200 Unhealthy – Everyone may begin to feel effects
  • 201–300 Very Unhealthy
  • 301+ Hazardous

How Weather Radar Works

Doppler weather radar detects precipitation and helps meteorologists track the movement and intensity of rain and storms. For dangerous weather, always use official National Weather Service warnings and alerts as the authoritative source.

Weather Radar and Forecast FAQs

What is Doppler weather radar?

Doppler radar uses radio waves to detect precipitation and estimate its movement. It is widely used to monitor rain, snow, thunderstorms, and other weather systems.

How often does weather radar update?

Radar products can update frequently, but timing varies by source and product. This website refreshes the national radar image automatically and links to official NWS radar for local verification.

How can I check weather by ZIP code?

Enter a five-digit US ZIP code in the search box. The site resolves the location and displays weather conditions and forecast information for that area.

What do radar colors mean?

Radar color scales generally represent precipitation intensity, but exact colors vary by radar product. Always check the legend of the specific radar image.

Where can I find severe weather alerts?

For official watches, warnings, and advisories, check the National Weather Service and local emergency-management authorities.
