2. Radar Data Is an Estimate, Not a Measurement

Radar does not measure rainfall or weather directly. It measures reflected energy, which is converted into estimates — for example, reflectivity is converted into an estimated rain rate using the Marshall–Palmer Z–R relationship. Hail, snow, ground clutter, and the distance from a radar station can all shift the estimate. Treat the maps as a guide to what is happening, not as an exact measurement.
